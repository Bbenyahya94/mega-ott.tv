Getting started

Your setup guide.

Most devices are ready in less than five minutes after your account is activated.

01

Receive your credentials

After purchase, you receive your username, password, and server URL by WhatsApp. Keep them private.

02

Install your player

Open the Downloads page, choose an app that matches your device, and install it from its official source.

03

Add your playlist

In your player, choose “Xtream Codes” or “Add user”, then enter your server URL, username and password exactly as received.

04

MAG / Enigma2 portal setup

Send support your device MAC address. We will authorize it and provide the portal URL. Restart your device after entering the portal URL.

Need a hand?

Message support with a screenshot of any error and the device/app you are using.
